This page explains how to request deletion of your **ListaNatin** account and
what happens to your data when you do. ListaNatin is provided by Earth Jan
Baquir Marzan.

## How to request deletion

Email **[earth.baquir.marzan@gmail.com](mailto:earth.baquir.marzan@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20ListaNatin%20account)**
with the subject line **"Delete my ListaNatin account."**

So we can find the right account, please include:

- the name on your ListaNatin account, and
- your mobile number, if you added and verified one in the app.

We'll confirm by reply and complete the deletion within **30 days** of confirming
your request.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we clear:

- your mobile number, and its verified status
- your profile photo
- your push-notification token and your notification preference
- the links to the sign-in provider you used

## What we keep, and why

We keep **your name** and **your paluwagan history** — your contributions, your
payouts, and your activity log entries.

This is not a technicality, and it's worth being plain about: a paluwagan is a
shared record. Other members' own histories reference yours. If we erased your
name and your contribution history, we would be putting gaps in records that
don't belong only to you — other people in your group would lose the record of
money they contributed or received. So those entries stay, tied to your name, as
part of the group's own history.

Everything else above is removed.

## One condition before we can delete

If you're currently an admin or a member of an **active** paluwagan, we can't
delete your account yet. Other members are mid-cycle and are relying on that
record while the paluwagan is running.

Once your paluwagan has been completed or cancelled, email us and we'll go ahead.
If you're not sure whether this applies to you, email us anyway and we'll check.

## A note on how ListaNatin works

ListaNatin keeps the record of a paluwagan; it never holds or moves anyone's
money. Every peso moves directly between members, outside the app. That means we
hold **no card numbers, no bank details, and no payment credentials** — there are
none to delete, because we never had them.
