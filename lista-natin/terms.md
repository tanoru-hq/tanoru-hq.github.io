## 2. Words We Use

This section explains the words used throughout these App Terms with a specific, capitalized meaning.

**ListaNatin** ("we," "us," "our") is the provider of the Service. **The Service** is the ListaNatin software itself: it records paluwagan arrangements, computes schedules and due dates from settings a Session Admin chose, displays what members enter, and sends notifications. **A User** is a real person the Service recognizes by an identity whose details stay fixed once set. **A Session Admin** is the User the Service identifies as the admin of a given paluwagan: they set that Session's contribution amount, frequency, and penalty; write that Session's own Terms and Conditions; approve or reject Contributions; and manage who is in it. A Session Admin owes no Contribution and is never a Session Participant. **A Session Participant** (a member of a paluwagan) is a contributor in a Session: they owe Contributions, may incur Penalties, and receive a Payout in payout order. A **Contribution** (a hulog) is a Session Participant's record that they paid what a cycle requires, submitted as a notification with an optional image as proof — not itself a transfer of money. A **Payout Record** (the record of a Payout, or sahod) is the Service's record that a Session's cycle has come due, naming who is next in payout order and an amount computed from that Session's settings. **Off-App Settlement** is the fact that every transfer of money in a paluwagan happens directly between members, outside the Service, through whatever channel they arrange themselves. A Session's own **Terms and Conditions** are the rules a Session Admin writes for their own paluwagan, shown in the app as "Rules ng paluwagan" — a different document from these App Terms, as Section 1 explains. Three shorter terms follow: a **Penalty** is what a Session Participant incurs for a late Contribution, sized by the penalty setting their Session Admin chose; a **Payout** is what a Payout Record represents — a Session Participant's turn in their Session's payout order; and an **Active Obligation** is the condition of a User still being responsible in an active Session, as admin or participant.

## 3. What ListaNatin Is — and What It Is Not

The Service records paluwagan arrangements: it computes schedules and due dates from the settings a Session Admin chose, displays what members enter, and sends notifications. That is the whole of what the Service does with money-related information.

The Service does not hold, receive, transmit, escrow, or guarantee funds, and has no payment integration of any kind. Every figure you see in the app — a Contribution, a Penalty, a Payout Record — is a number the Service calculated from settings a Session Admin entered, not a balance the Service is holding on anyone's behalf.

The Service also runs on third-party infrastructure (Firebase and Google Cloud) that ListaNatin does not operate directly, so outages or changes on that infrastructure are outside ListaNatin's control.

## 4. Money Moves Between Members, Not Through Us (Off-App Settlement)

Every transfer of money in a paluwagan happens directly between members — from one Session Participant to another, or between a Session Participant and their Session Admin — outside the Service, through whatever channel they arrange themselves, such as GCash or cash. The Service is never a step in that transfer: there is no processor, no custody, and no route through which money passes through ListaNatin. Money never enters the Service's control at any point, however briefly.

Instead, the Service records what members tell it. A Contribution is a Session Participant's notification that they paid, optionally evidenced by an uploaded image. Approval is the Session Admin's own attestation that they received that payment — their judgment call, not a verification the Service performs. Neither a Contribution nor its approval is a transaction executed, observed, or verified by the Service; both are records of what a member reported, nothing more.

## 7. Roles in a Paluwagan

A paluwagan on ListaNatin has two roles. Every Session has exactly one Session Admin — the User the Service identifies as that Session's admin — and any number of Session Participants.

**Session Admin.** The Session Admin sets the Session's contribution amount, frequency, and Penalty when the Session is created; these cannot be changed afterward. They write the Session's own Terms and Conditions, approve or reject each Contribution reported to them, can remove or replace a Session Participant, and can close, reactivate, or cancel the Session. A Session Admin owes no Contribution of their own — the Session Admin is never a Session Participant in their own Session.

**Session Participant.** A Session Participant owes Contributions on the schedule the Session Admin set, can incur a Penalty for a late one, and receives a Payout in the Session's payout order. They cannot leave a Session on their own — only the Session Admin can remove them — and cannot deactivate or delete their User while an Active Obligation stands.

## 8. Contributions, Approval, and Penalties

A Contribution is a record that a Session Participant reports to their Session Admin — a statement that a payment was made, not the payment itself. The money involved has already moved directly between the Session Participant and the Session Admin, off the Service, as an Off-App Settlement.

Only a Session Admin's approval of a Contribution satisfies that cycle's due-date obligation. Approval is the Session Admin's own judgment call about a payment they say they received — it is not something the Service checks or verifies in any way.

The Service computes due dates, the "late" status, and Penalty figures from the settings the Session Admin chose when the Session was created. They organize the paluwagan; they do not create, guarantee, or collect a debt, on ListaNatin's behalf or anyone else's.

ListaNatin does not decide whether a payment was actually made, and does not reverse, refund, or adjust a Session Admin's approval or rejection of a Contribution. If a Session Participant and a Session Admin disagree about whether a payment happened, that is a dispute between them.

## 9. Payout Records

A Payout Record is the Service's record that a Session's cycle has come due, naming which Session Participant is next in the payout order, together with an amount.

That amount is calculated, not held. It is the Session's contribution amount multiplied by the number of active Session Participants, regardless of how many Contributions for that cycle were actually approved. If a Session Participant misses a Contribution, the resulting Penalty adds to this figure rather than reducing it. A number that goes up when someone fails to pay cannot be a statement about money ListaNatin holds. It is an entitlement calculation — what the Session's rules say the next Session Participant in line is owed — not a bank balance or a promise from ListaNatin.

A Payout Record has no receipt, confirmation, or status field behind it. The Service has no way to know whether a Payout was actually handed over between members, and cannot represent that a Payout failed even when one did. Where the Service or this document describes a Payout as "released," that means a Payout Record was written and the relevant members were notified — nothing more, and never that ListaNatin moved, held, or delivered any money.

Your User can move through three states: active, deactivated, and deleted. Deletion does clear a short, specific list of fields, set out below.

You can deactivate or delete your User from within the Service, as long as you don't have an Active Obligation outstanding — for example, a cycle that is due with no approved Contribution from you yet. When you delete your User, six specific fields are cleared: your mobile number, whether that mobile number was verified, your profile photo, your push-notification token, your notification preference, and any sign-in providers linked to it. Deleting your User does not block you from signing back in. You can sign in again at any time with the same identity, and you'll be recognized as the same User.

A problem report you've submitted stays in our records for troubleshooting even after you delete your User, but is no longer linked to it.
